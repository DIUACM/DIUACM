# Official Rules and Guidelines for ACM Lab
**Daffodil International University**  
**Effective Date:** 10th November 2025

This rulebook establishes the standards of conduct, cleanliness, and professional use required within the DIU ACM Lab — a space dedicated to excellence in programming.

---

## 1. Purpose of the ACM Lab

The DIU ACM Lab serves as a professional environment for competitive programming, algorithmic discussion, and collaborative training among DIU students. To protect its resources and ensure fairness, discipline, and productivity, all members must adhere to the rules outlined in this document.

- The lab is intended exclusively for ACM and programming-related activities.
- Every member must uphold professionalism, respect, and responsibility in the lab.

---

## 2. Cleanliness and Equipment Care

Maintaining a clean and safe environment is vital for both hardware protection and personal comfort.

- **Shoes Off:** Remove footwear before entering. Place them neatly on the provided shoe rack.
- **Keep the Space Organized:** Avoid leaving papers, notebooks, or personal items unattended. Dispose of trash responsibly.
- **Report Spills or Damage:** Immediately inform the ACM lab supervisor about any spill, malfunction, or safety concern.
- **No Food Allowed:** Food and snacks are strictly prohibited. Only snacks during any contest are allowed.

---

## 3. Professional Conduct and Focus

The ACM Lab is a high-concentration environment. Members are expected to maintain focus and minimize distractions.

- Maintain a quiet, respectful atmosphere. Conversations must be brief and academic in nature.
- Mobile phones should remain on silent or vibrate mode. Calls must be taken outside the lab.
- Use of headphones is allowed for tutorials, problem-solving videos — provided the volume is inaudible to others.

### Strictly Prohibited:
- Playing non-educational or entertainment games.
- Streaming movies, sports, or unrelated videos.
- Browsing social media excessively.
- Working on anything other than problem-solving tasks.
- Sleeping after 07:30 AM (In case of staying overnight in the Lab)

---

## 4. System and Hardware Policy

The computers and network systems in the ACM Lab are provided for competitive programming and research only. Members must use them responsibly.

- **Do Not Modify Hardware:** Never unplug, swap, or move components such as PCs, monitors, keyboards, or cables without permission.
- **No External Devices:** Do not connect personal hardware (USB drives, external hard disks, etc.) unless authorized by the lab advisor.
- **Authorized Software Only:** Installation of any unapproved applications, plugins, or games is strictly forbidden.
- **Logout Discipline:** Always log out after your session and never leave a workstation unlocked or unattended. Using Incognito/Private mode for temporary login is suggested.

---

## 5. Access and Security

Access to the ACM Lab is restricted to authorized members only. Security and respect for others' property are mandatory.

- **Authorized Entry Only:** Access is limited to ACM trainees and approved DIU students.
- **Personal Belongings:** Keep personal items secure. The university or ACM lab will not be responsible for lost property.
- **Respect Property:** Do not mark, write on, or damage any desk, chair, or device.
- **Monitoring:** The lab may be under supervision or CCTV monitoring to ensure safety and accountability.

---

## 6. Accountability and Consequences

Every member is expected to follow these rules with integrity. Violations will lead to disciplinary action depending on severity.

- Minor infractions will result in verbal or written warnings.
- Repeated or serious violations may lead to temporary or permanent suspension of ACM Lab access.
- Damage to university property due to negligence or misconduct will incur repair costs and possible administrative penalties.

---

## Final Note

- Rules and regulations can be changed and modified anytime by the DIU ACM Advisory Panel.
- The DIU ACM Lab represents the heart of DIU's programming community. Its environment of discipline, respect, and dedication enables excellence in national and international contests. By following these rules, every member contributes to that spirit of excellence.
